Frequently Asked Questions

What are your opening hours?

We're open Monday to Friday from 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and Saturday to Sunday from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM.

Do you accept reservations?

Yes! We highly recommend making a reservation, especially on weekends. You can make a reservation through our website or by calling us directly.

Do you offer vegetarian or vegan options?

Absolutely! Our menu features a wide variety of vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options. Please inform your server about any dietary restrictions.

Do you cater for private events?

Yes! We offer private dining and catering services for special events, corporate gatherings, weddings, and more. Contact our events team for more information.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), debit cards, cash, and digital payment methods including PayPal, Google Pay, and Apple Pay.
